Our Mission

Our Mission is to renovate spaces that satisfy more than just our physical needs. Green Construction is an act of renovating our consciousness and maintain a harmonious relationship with nature.

Our respect for the environment was the fundamental basis for a harmonious life in ancient cultures, each time we take something from Mother Earth (Pachamama), we must replace what we have taken.

In the wasteful and polluting industry of construction, LD Creative Renovations makes an effort to reduce its carbon footprint by using green technology such as reclaimed wood, natural paints and finishes among other products. In addition, LD Creative Renovations is currently participating in the One Million Trees Volunteering Program in New York.

“The nation behaves well if it treats the natural resources as assets which it must turn over to the next generation increased, and not impaired, in value.”

—Theodore Roosevelt
