Community Service

Deforestation, the clearance of naturally occurring forests, is a significant danger facing our environment. The removal of trees without sufficient reforestation has resulted in various adverse effects, including extinction, changes to climatic conditions, desertification, and the displacement of indigenous people. In our dedication to protecting the environment, LD Creative Renovations will plant a tree for every project we undertake, as a counter to the amount of wood we use in construction.

Here are some other ways that you can give back to the environment:
