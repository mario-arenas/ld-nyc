Project Description

This striking completely renovated Manhattan duplex featured natural products including Mahogany wood paneling, unique multicolored glass work, stone floors and tinted Venetian plaster.

Harper Collins CEO Jane Friedman’s striking completely renovated Manhattan duplex feels like a mix between luxury mansion and museum. Each room is a design and architecture marvel, expressing a unique personality of its own. Infuses aesthetics with everyday function. A subtle mix of Jerusalem stone floors with tinted Venetian plaster highlights the client’s collection of art and vintage furniture. The stunning staircase integrates colored lights into ribbon mahogany wood crafted by hand into artfully matching veneers with a sleek, yatch-style finish.
—Featured in “Home by Design” Magazine
