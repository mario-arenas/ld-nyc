Project Description

Innovation and complete renovation transformed this unit in a 1900 building into a unique, contemporary luxury residence. Two loft floors and a terrace were merged into a duplex unit with a penthouse, connected by a hand-made maple staircase. Due to the space contrast and the addition of an ingenious maple louver system and magnificent architectural woodwork with a custom satin stain finish, this residence truly has a personality of its own.
