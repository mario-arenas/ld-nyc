Feldschuh Loft

Raw loft space in the heart of Soho, this loft embraces an eclectic and sophisticated fashion statement with a New York attitude. This renovation for Michael Feldusch included a flawless restoration of landmark antique wood windows and a pain-staking restoration of a richly textured tin set ceiling. Showing the restored existing wood floor and columns and keeping the historic beauty of the building while minimally impacting the environment.
