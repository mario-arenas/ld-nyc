Kitchen Apartment

Enlightened spacious love-filled home. The complete renovation of a loft space for Robert Kitchen in SoHo, where art and everyday life are inextricably linked. This stylish 5,000 square foot location is highlighted by a Bohemian-style hallway combining slate and wood. In addition, the bath is a masterpiece of remarkable tile work, including travertine floor tiles with inserts of onyx and French stone and hand-made clay wall tiles with glass inserts.
