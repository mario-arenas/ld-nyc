Miles Apartment

Guided by divine of the project and identity of the clients. We transformed an early 20th century loft in the heart of tribeca. It was one open space with one bedroom converted into two bedrooms with an open living room where the sun shine flow. Flawless restoration, cherry wood cabinetry, concrete counter-tops, custom marble rain shower with a custom designed drainage. “Honor the past marble reflect the soul of timeless nature.” Restoration of the wood floor, the result is a beautiful work that delights senses and engaged mind.
